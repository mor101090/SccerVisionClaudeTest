Match facts

This is the real C# match engine (the same code that runs inside Unity), recorded at seed and replayed at its real pace. The clock runs 4.5× faster than real time, so 45 minutes take 10. The Unity build draws the same match with full graphics and sound. The first match is staged to contain one red card, one light injury and one serious injury with a substitution; find them under "Key moments". The settings (gear) choose the weather, wind, pitch upkeep, day or night, grass pattern, net and team colours and the match pace; rain, snow, wind and a worn pitch replay the match as the engine plays it in those conditions.
